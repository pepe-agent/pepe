# Release Notes 0.17.1

A release focused on removing unnecessary confusion from the dashboard.

---

## 🐛 Fixes

### The dashboard no longer shows a confusing "localhost" in the sidebar

The dashboard's sidebar always displayed the word "localhost," even when you accessed Pepe from a different address (a different machine, behind a reverse proxy, or on a local network). That didn't match what was in your browser's address bar and caused confusion.

The sidebar now shows just "Pepe" and the running version number, without a label that doesn't match what you're actually looking at.

📍 *Where to see it: the dashboard's left sidebar*
